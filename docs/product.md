# Product claims & limitations

## What Thunder is

A pre-publication **audience intelligence and scenario-testing** workspace for creators who have historical comments and a draft they want to pressure-test.

## Honest claims (use these in pitch)

- Thunder runs a **grounded scenario simulation** based on patterns in the creator’s supplied audience data.
- Audience segments are **evidence-backed** with validated comment IDs.
- Diagnostic scores are **deterministic** from constrained qualitative factors.
- The optimized carousel resolves **segment trade-offs** without inventing unsupported claims.

## Non-claims (do not say)

- We predict real humans perfectly
- We know exact future views
- We guarantee virality
- Simulated segments are actual named users
- Scores are objective scientific measurements

## Out of scope / deferred (say honestly)

- Auth, database, social network features, direct publishing
- **Poke this segment** interactive chat (wanted; deferred — not core)
- Small mobile companion app (wanted; deferred — not enough time)
- Firecrawl / ElevenLabs inside the product (ElevenLabs OK for video voiceover only)
